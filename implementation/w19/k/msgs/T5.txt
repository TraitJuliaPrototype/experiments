gf, datatype: a membership invalidates as a method does, and a membership taken back

A membership makes the methods at its set apply to the member, as a method definition makes itself
apply. This makes it invalidate the code and the dispatch caches that relied on the earlier answer,
as the definition of that method at the member would.

- The walk (`jl_membership_dispatch_walk`), run by the declaration under the world counter's lock
  in the entry's world: for every method at the set or at a set the member joins (the list by set),
  the method's insertion is replayed with the member in place of the set
  (`membership_subst_sig`; below a position's top too: in a union, a tuple's component, an
  intersection, the bound a type declares for its parameter, `membership_subst_nested`). The
  replay runs the insertion's own code (`invalidate_intersecting`, the body of
  `jl_method_table_activate` it shares): the specializations and the callers whose selected method
  changes are invalidated, and the dispatch caches lowered. Only the method's own function is
  visited, by an intersection with `Tuple{F, Vararg{Any}}`.
- What a membership does not change keeps its code: a method that names the set only as an
  invariant parameter (`Vector{X}`) is not replayed; a call that matched the replayed method in the
  world before the membership (a call at `Any`, at an abstract type that met the set) has the same
  matches after it (`replay_matched_before`); the wide instance of another method stays.
- A method whose variable has a lower bound at a covariant place (`h(::Ph{T}) where T >: S`) is
  selected, once `S` joins `X`, at a type that names the set (`Ph{X}`): there a type under the
  member, not the set, carries the membership. The list by set keeps such methods under the key
  `nothing` (twenty of Base's methods: `T >: Missing`, `T >: Nothing`); the walk and the removal
  walk replay each whose bound meets the member at the method's own signature
  (`lower_bound_targets`), and a match's worlds are narrowed by the entries of the signature's lower
  bounds (`jl_sig_lower_bounds`).
- An entry at instances of its own variables (a member of the bare set, every instance) reaches a
  method at one instance where the two meet (`membership_param_meets`).
- A shape that one substituted signature cannot state (the set under a `Vararg`, in a lower bound,
  in several places below a position's top) takes the conservative path: the method's own instances
  and callers end, and every ground specialization of the table that the method matches in the
  entry's world (`membership_shadow_visitor`). Where a bound the signature sets names the set, the
  method is replayed at its own signature.
- A dispatch backedge that keeps the call's own signature (a call that reached a wider instance)
  is judged at that signature.
- What the membership ends of the code compiled by facts in the deep class is kept, for a tool that
  asks (`jl_deep_ending_push("membership", entry)`), as a definition's is.
- A membership taken back (`jl_delete_membership`): its entry ends in the current world, and the
  removal walk ends what relied on it: each method at the set is replayed at the member as if
  deleted there (its instances at the member and their callers, also the callers of an instance with
  no code, as an `applicable` or `hasmethod` folded through the membership names it, a caller of a wider instance whose
  call selected it at the member, the cache entries, a call at the member that found no method or
  an ambiguity). A call at the member that selected the member's own method keeps its code. A member
  by its declared supertype cannot be taken back, nor while a package image is built.
- Counters of the walk for tests (`jl_membership_stat`) and a line per declaration on
  `JULIA_MEMBERSHIP_LOG`.
