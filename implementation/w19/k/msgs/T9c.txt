datatype, gf: a strict mode for a trait library, which refuses a membership that makes methods ambiguous

A library's strict mode (`jl_set_strict_memberships`) turns the ambiguity report at a membership into
a refusal: the declaration asks, in the entry's world before the counter advances to it and under the
world counter's lock, whether the entry makes methods ambiguous (`jl_membership_ambiguities`, the
search through `ml_matches_i`, which takes the world past the counter there); a finding retires the
entry before it begins and raises an error that names each ambiguity as the report's warning does.
At an image's activation the findings are kept as messages for the library
(`jl_take_activation_ambiguities`) in place of the warning.

For the library's check of a pirate's membership: what a membership would replay
(`jl_membership_replay_sigs`, also the methods whose lower bound the member meets, each at its own
signature) and its method's signature (`jl_membership_method_sig`).

Test: test/subtype.jl, "a strict mode".
