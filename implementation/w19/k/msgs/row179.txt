Compiler: a call's edge at a top-level union is split, each split with its own edge

A call with one fully covering match that reaches an instance wider than its signature keeps the
call's signature in its dispatch edge and its backedge (the call-signature backedge, earlier in this
series). Where that signature has a union at its top level, as a call of `!==`, `TypeVar` or
`UnionAll` matched at its own argument types has (`(Union{Nothing, Int64}, Nothing)` in
`in(::Char, ::String)`), the edge named the union. It is now split as inference splits a call,
within the facts inference's union-splitting cost (`callsig_splits`), and each split is recorded
with its own edge: each a fact signature where its types are concrete, so the deep class can admit
the caller. The union of the splits is the call's signature, so a method defined later ends the
caller exactly where it did.

Test: `Compiler/test/inference.jl` (the splits of a signature; none past the cost, which leaves the
largest union out of its product); the gate `f4_g2_bit`.
